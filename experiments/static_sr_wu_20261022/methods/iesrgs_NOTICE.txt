IE-SRGS-on-Wu-4DGS source attribution
===================================

The supervision semantics, model settings, Mip filtering equations and RADE
interface are adapted from the official IE-SRGS implementation:
  https://github.com/ZheXues/IE-SRGS
  commit 74d60d5bb0f49974ac0efa3497dbbd9e246480f4
  Xiang Feng, Tieshi Zhong, Shuo Chang, Weiliu Wang, Chengkai Wang,
  Yifei Chen, Tongyu Hu, Yuhe Wang, Zhenzhong Kuang, Xuefei Yin, Yanming Zhu.
  AAAI 2026, DOI: 10.1609/aaai.v40i5.37398

The author repository builds on Mip-Splatting and 3D Gaussian Splatting.
Its LICENSE.md states a non-commercial research/evaluation license from
Inria and MPII. Keep the original LICENSE.md with any local source/build
distribution and comply with it. This notice does not relicense the author
implementation. No upstream CUDA source snapshot or pretrained weights are
included in this project's public source package.

The adapter explicitly replaces static Gaussians with Wu 4DGaussians effective
states and time-indexed sampling. It must not be represented as the original
static benchmark result. Original rasterization and model implementations are
loaded as external dependencies from separately versioned local checkouts.

The local CUDA 12.8 compatibility patch adds only the missing standard header
<cstdint> in cuda_rasterizer/rasterizer_impl.h. The original checkout is kept
unchanged. Binary provenance and this patch's hash are recorded in the local
build_receipt.json; GPU correctness is a separate acceptance step.

SwinIR: Jingyun Liang et al., https://github.com/JingyunLiang/SwinIR
Depth Anything V2: Lihe Yang et al., https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2
Their source and pretrained-model licenses remain applicable. In particular,
the selected Depth Anything V2-Large weights must not be silently replaced
with the Small model to avoid the selected model's availability or license.

The full author repository license is preserved at the locally cloned
tmp/static_sr_wu_20261022/upstream/iesrgs/LICENSE.md and can be obtained at:
https://github.com/ZheXues/IE-SRGS/blob/74d60d5bb0f49974ac0efa3497dbbd9e246480f4/LICENSE.md
